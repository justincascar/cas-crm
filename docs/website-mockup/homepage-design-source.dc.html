How it works

From the roadside to back on the road

You’ll get a text or email at every stage, so you always know where things are.

  1. STEP 1

    Tell us what happened

    Call us or use the secure online form, with photos from your phone.

  2. STEP 2

    We recover your car

  3. STEP 3

    You stay on the road

Just happened?

What to do at the scene

Keep calm and keep safe. These steps make your claim much easier.

Need recovery? Call 01792 341069
  1. 1Make sure everyone is safe
    Call 999 if anyone is hurt or the road is blocked.
  2. 2Swap details
    Name, address, registration and insurer of the other driver.
  3. 3Take photos
    Both vehicles, number plates, the road and the damage.
  4. 4Note any witnesses
    A name and phone number is enough.
  5. 5Tell your insurer
    Your policy asks you to report it, even if you use us.
  6. 6Call CAS
    We’ll take it from there.

Your fault or not, we can help

Not your fault

  • Recovery and secure storage if your car can’t be driven
  • A comparable replacement car while yours is off the road
  • Repairs agreed with the other driver’s insurer
  • We pursue the costs from the other driver’s insurer

Your fault

  • Recovery and secure storage if your car can’t be driven
  • Repairs agreed with your own insurer
  • A courtesy car while yours is fixed, where available
  • Your policy excess is paid when you collect your car

Not sure whose fault it was? Call 01792 341069 and we’ll talk it through honestly.

Straight talking

Your options after a non-fault accident

You have a choice. Here’s how they compare, so you can decide what suits you.

Claim on your own policy

Your insurer handles it. You may pay your excess, and any courtesy car depends on your cover.

Claim from the other insurer yourself

You deal with the other driver’s insurer directly and arrange recovery, repairs and transport yourself.

We do it all for you

Use CAS

We arrange recovery, a replacement car and repairs on credit, then claim the costs from the other driver’s insurer. You sign our agreements, which explain the charges and when you could become responsible for them.

Good to know Our replacement car agreement is not regulated by the Consumer Credit Act. If the other insurer disputes the claim, you may need to help us recover the costs. We explain this before you sign.
