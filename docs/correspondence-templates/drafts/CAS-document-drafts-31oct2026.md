## Which letters go out

The initial letter to the third-party insurer goes out only on non-fault, split and disputed claims, never on a fault claim.

| Liability status | To insurers | To the client | Hire |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Non-fault | Initial letter to the third-party insurer (existing); notification to the client's own insurer (Draft 4) | Welcome; client instruction and authority (Draft 1) | Credit hire agreement with the Storage & Recovery page |
| Fault | Letter to the client's own insurer asking for the claim reference and network code (Draft 3) | Welcome (fault wording); repair authority (Draft 2) | Courtesy car agreement (Draft 7), not credit hire |
| Split, for example 50/50 | Split-liability letter to the third-party insurer (Draft 5a); letter to the client's own insurer (Draft 3) | Split-liability explanation (Draft 5b); client instruction and authority (Draft 1) | Justin decides per file |
| Disputed or not yet known | Letter asking the third-party insurer for its position (Draft 6); notification to the client's own insurer (Draft 4) | Welcome; client instruction and authority (Draft 1) | Justin decides per file |

When the liability status changes, the CRM offers the letters for the new status and keeps the old ones on the file.

## Draft 1: Client instruction and authority (non-fault, split or disputed)

**Client Instruction and Authority — [CAS reference]**

Client: [client name], [client address]. Vehicle: [make and model] [registration]. Accident: [accident date] at [accident location], involving [third-party registration].

1. **Instruction.** I instruct Complete Accident Solutions Ltd ("CAS") to manage my claim arising from this accident. This may include recovery and storage of my vehicle, a replacement vehicle if I need one, an engineer's inspection, repairs, and pursuing my losses from the person responsible and their insurer.
2. **Authority.** I authorise CAS to deal on my behalf with the third party's insurer and representatives, my own insurer, engineers, repairers and the police, and to request and receive information about the accident and my claim.
3. **My information.** The information I have given CAS is true to the best of my knowledge. I will tell CAS straight away if anything changes, or if any insurer, solicitor or claims company contacts me about this accident.
4. **Help with the claim.** I will provide my driving licence, insurance certificate, logbook (V5C) and any photographs or witness details I have. I will make my vehicle available for inspection, and give a signed statement if asked.
5. **Charges.** The charges for recovery, storage, hire and repairs are set out in the separate agreements I sign with CAS. This form does not change those agreements.
6. **Payments.** If an insurer pays me directly for a loss CAS has provided or paid for, I will tell CAS and pass that payment to CAS within [7] days.
7. **Offers.** I will not accept an offer, or settle any part of this claim that covers services provided by CAS, without first telling CAS.
8. **My own insurer.** I authorise CAS to notify my own insurer of the accident. This is not a claim on my policy unless I ask for one.
9. **My information and privacy.** I have been given CAS's privacy notice, which explains how CAS uses my personal information, including my driving licence check.
10. **Starting straight away.** [ ] I ask CAS to start providing its services immediately, within the cancellation period. I have been given CAS's cancellation notice.

Signed: ______________ Name: [client name] Date: [date] Signed [on screen / on paper]

Solicitor: clauses 4, 6, 7 and 10; whether a separate assignment or mandate is wanted.

## Draft 2: Client repair authority (fault claim)

**Repair Authority — [CAS reference]**

Client: [client name], [client address]. Vehicle: [make and model] [registration]. My insurer: [own insurer], policy [policy number].

1. **Repairs.** I ask Complete Accident Solutions Ltd ("CAS") to repair the accident damage to my vehicle, and to claim the cost from my insurer under my policy.
2. **My insurer.** I authorise CAS to deal with my insurer, its engineers and its approved-repairer network about the repair, and to send them estimates, photographs and invoices.
3. **Excess.** I will pay my policy excess of £[excess] to CAS before my vehicle is released, unless my insurer tells CAS the excess is waived.
4. **Work not covered.** If my insurer will not pay for part of the work (for example betterment, damage not caused by this accident, or extra work I ask for), CAS will tell me the cost in writing first. That work is only done if I agree to pay for it.
5. **If the repair does not go ahead.** If my insurer declines the claim or decides my vehicle is a total loss, CAS will tell me. Recovery and storage charges are set out in the Storage & Recovery Agreement I sign.
6. **Courtesy car.** Any courtesy car is provided under a separate Courtesy Car Agreement.
7. **Collecting my vehicle.** When the repair is finished, CAS will tell me and I will arrange to collect my vehicle within [Justin to set] days.
8. **My information and privacy.** I have been given CAS's privacy notice.
9. **Starting straight away.** [ ] I ask CAS to start immediately, within the cancellation period. I have been given CAS's cancellation notice.

Signed: ______________ Name: [client name] Date: [date]

## Draft 5: Split liability (for example 50/50)

Both letters need Justin's approval before sending (admissions and the client's own liability).

**5a — Letter to the third-party insurer**

Dear Sir / Madam,

**Our Client: [client name] — Your Insured: [third-party registration] — Accident Date: [accident date]**

Your Reference: [third-party claim reference] — Our Reference: [CAS reference]

We note your proposal that liability for this accident be shared [their %] / [our %]. We are taking our client's instructions and will respond shortly. Nothing in this letter is an admission of liability.

In the meantime, please confirm in writing the basis of your proposal and any evidence you rely on, including any statement from your insured, dash-cam footage or witness details.

Yours faithfully,
[Handler name], Complete Accident Solutions Ltd

**5b — Explanation to the client (letter or email)**

Dear [client name],

The other driver's insurer has suggested that liability for your accident should be shared, [their %] / [our %]. We have not agreed to this.

If a split is agreed, their insurer would pay only [their %] of each part of your claim: repairs, recovery, storage and hire. The rest may be claimable from your own insurer if you have comprehensive cover, or may otherwise be your responsibility under the agreements you signed with us.

Before anything is agreed, we will call you to talk through your options, including whether your replacement vehicle should continue. Please call us on [CAS phone] if you have any questions.

Kind regards, [Handler name]

Solicitor: the client's exposure for the unrecovered share and how to explain it fairly. Justin: whether CAS offers credit hire on split-liability files at all.
