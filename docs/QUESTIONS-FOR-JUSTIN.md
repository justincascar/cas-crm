16. **What the card fee is worked out on.** The fee is the Settings percentage of the net amount being paid. Please confirm, or say if the fee should be worked out differently (for example on the gross).
17. **A signed note when the hire car is swapped.** Swapping the car mid-hire keeps one continuous hire and does not produce a new agreement page, because the supplied terms define the Hire Vehicle as including "any vehicle with which We replace it during the Rental Period". Default: no new signature; the swap and that reason go on the history. Do you want the client to sign a short vehicle-change note as well? If so, please supply the wording.
18. **An overpayment.** When more than is outstanding arrives on a head, staff confirm it and it is recorded on that head, shown as overpaid, flagged for review, and never set against any other head. Default: a person decides whether to refund it or hold it. Is there a rule you want (for example, refund within so many days)?
